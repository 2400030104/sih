# MoSPI / IPMD Central Sector Infrastructure Monitoring Guidelines

**Document Category**: Policy & Standard Operating Procedure  
**Issuing Authority**: Infrastructure and Project Monitoring Division (IPMD), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India  
**Target Scope**: Central Sector Projects costing ₹150 crore and above  

---

## 1. Project Monitoring Mandate & Thresholds
1. **Cost Threshold**: IPMD monitors all Central Sector Infrastructure Projects with an approved sanctioned cost of ₹150 crore and above.
2. **Flash Reporting**: Projects costing ₹1000 crore and above are subjected to Flash Report monitoring on a monthly cycle.
3. **OCMS & PAIMANA Migration**: Online Computerized Monitoring System (OCMS) established digital monthly reporting across key metrics: original sanction, approved revision, cumulative expenditure, physical progress %, financial progress %, milestone tracking, and delay causality. PAIMANA enhances this with real-time exception reporting.

---

## 2. Key Monitoring Parameters
1. **Physical Progress**: Measure of actual on-ground civil, electrical, and structural completion as a percentage against approved scope.
2. **Financial Progress / Expenditure**: Ratio of cumulative expenditure incurred to the approved sanctioned cost.
3. **Physical-Financial Progress Gap**: A significant divergence where Financial Progress exceeds Physical Progress by >15% indicates potential cost inflation, advance payments without commensurate physical realization, or mobilization bottlenecks.
4. **Schedule Variance**: Difference between planned completion target date and revised/estimated completion date. Schedule slippage exceeding 90 days triggers an administrative alert.

---

## 3. Cost Overrun Classification
1. **Low Variance (0% - 10%)**: Managed under contingency provisions of administrative approval.
2. **Moderate Overrun (10% - 25%)**: Requires Revised Cost Estimate (RCE-I) with departmental standing committee review.
3. **Critical Overrun (>25%)**: Mandates Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) or Public Investment Board (PIB) approval, comprehensive appraisal, and inter-ministerial justification.

---

## 4. Delay Factors & Accountability
Primary reasons for project delay recognized by IPMD:
- Land acquisition disputes and right-of-way (RoW) clearance delays.
- Statutory clearances (environmental, forest, wildlife, and railway safety).
- Utility shifting (electrical lines, pipelines, telecom cables).
- Contractual and vendor disputes, slow mobilization by EPC contractors.
- Geotechnical surprises, unexpected geological strata in tunnel and bridge works.
- Law and order / local public agitation.
- Inadequate fund allocation / state government counterpart funding delays.

---

## 5. Milestone Management
- All projects must identify critical path milestones with planned and actual completion dates.
- Delay in a **Critical** milestone automatically cascades risk to downstream packages and demands an immediate Milestone Recovery Schedule (MRS) from the executing agency within 14 calendar days.
