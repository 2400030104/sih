# Infrastructure Project Recovery & Intervention Guidelines

**Document Category**: Policy & Decision Support Guide  
**Subject**: Remedial Actions, Acceleration Strategies, and Escalation Mechanisms  
**Applicability**: Delayed or At-Risk Mega Projects under MoSPI Monitoring  

---

## 1. Schedule Recovery Protocols (SCHEDULE_RECOVERY)
When a project incurs critical schedule slippage (>90 days) or physical lag (>15%), the project director must evaluate the following recovery options:

1. **Crash Program / Parallel Execution**: Fast-track non-dependent packages (e.g., executing signaling and electrification in parallel with track laying rather than sequentially).
2. **Double-Shift / Multi-Shift Mobilization**: Mandate 24/7 or double-shift operations on critical-path civil structures (viaducts, tunnels, bridges) with adequate night illumination and safety supervision.
3. **Resource Augmentation**: Deploy additional specialized equipment (e.g., Launching Gantries, Tunnel Boring Machines, Heavy Paving Trains) funded through incentive mechanisms.
4. **Target Recalibration**: Re-sequence milestones without moving the final commissioning date, focusing resources strictly on critical-path activities.

---

## 2. Cost Control & Containment Protocols (COST_CONTROL)
When cost overrun risk exceeds 60% or expenditure growth diverges from physical output:

1. **Value Engineering Review**: Re-evaluate design specifications for non-core project amenities without compromising structural integrity or design life.
2. **Package Descoping & Decoupling**: If auxiliary packages are inflating costs, decouple non-critical components into separate phase-2 contracts.
3. **Material Price Escalation Audit**: Verify whether price variation claims match statutory indices (WPI/CPI) and contract formulas.
4. **Contingency Fund Freeze**: Impose strict departmental approval thresholds for drawing on unallocated project contingencies.

---

## 3. Contractor Review & Enforcement (CONTRACTOR_REVIEW)
1. **Notice to Correct (Clause-based)**: Issue formal contractual notices under FIDIC / Standard EPC conditions for failure to achieve milestone targets.
2. **Liquidated Damages (LD) Assessment**: Compute LD provisions where delays are solely attributable to contractor default without excusable time extensions.
3. **Sub-contracting / Step-in Rights**: Invoke contractual step-in provisions to assign lagging specialized sub-packages to supplemental vendors at the primary contractor's risk and cost.
4. **Joint Venture Escalation**: Summon parent company directors of joint venture partners for formal execution commitments.

---

## 4. Land Acquisition & Inter-Agency Coordination (RESOURCE_REVIEW)
1. **District Administration Task Force**: Establish a dedicated district-level coordination cell headed by the District Collector (DC) / District Magistrate (DM) for weekly grievance resolution.
2. **Direct Purchase / Enhanced Compensation**: Utilize direct purchase schemes under state land acquisition policies to bypass contested court litigation.
3. **Inter-Ministerial Committee Escalation**: Escalate forest clearance, railway crossing (ROB/RUB), defense land transfer, and environmental clearance bottlenecks to the Cabinet Secretariat PRAGATI monitoring platform.
